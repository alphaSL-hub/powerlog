What's inside

Built for the mid-set decision.

Every screen in PowerLog is designed to get out of your way while you're under the bar, then hand you the full picture the moment you rack it.

Workout & routine management

Build fully customized routines and assign exercises to match your training split. Log every set, rep, and weight through an interface built for speed — designed to keep up with you mid-set, not slow you down. In the mood to freestyle? Drop straight into Free Workout Mode and log a session with no routine set up first.

Progressive overload, tracked automatically

Health & body tracking

Go beyond the barbell. Log body weight, body fat percentage, and measurements for your chest, arms, waist, and thighs. Update only the metrics you care about — PowerLog's smart validation carries the rest over for you. See your BMI on a live gauge and stay on top of hydration with the built-in water intake tracker.

One log, every metric that matters

Hall of Fame & analytics

PowerLog automatically detects and records your all-time heaviest lift for every exercise — no manual entry required. Explore trend charts across 7 days, 1 month, and 6 months, plus spline area charts that show how each muscle group has grown over time.

Your PRs, catalogued the moment you hit them

Built-in notepad

Keep workout notes, diet tweaks, supplement schedules, and personal reminders in one place — right next to your training data, so nothing gets lost across five different apps.

Everything you need, one app

100% offline & privacy-first

Your data never leaves your device. PowerLog stores everything locally with SQLite — no accounts, no sign-ups, no data harvesting. What you log stays yours, completely.

No servers to trust. None involved.

Backup & restore

Switching phones doesn't mean starting over. Export your entire database to your Downloads folder whenever you want, then restore it on a new device and pick up exactly where you left off — zero data loss.

The fine print, made simple

No cloud. No excuses.

Everything stays on your phone

PowerLog stores your training and body data locally with SQLite. There's no account to create and nothing syncing to a server — your numbers belong to you, not a database somewhere else.

Export a backup, anytime

Save your full database as a backup file to your Downloads folder whenever you like. Getting a new phone? Restore it and every set, rep, and PR comes with you.
